Teaching modules

Six modules. One per practitioner.

Each module is taught by a single practitioner currently active in that specific area. You apply for the one that fits your expertise.

Financial Planning Basics
Ideal if you're a: CFP, financial planner, IFA
Budgeting frameworks, net worth, emergency funds. Teaching people to get their foundation right before they invest anything.
Investment Basics
Ideal if you're a: Wealth advisor, RM, distributor
Mutual funds, SIPs, equity vs. debt allocation. You've had this conversation with clients — now have it with people who are just starting.
Market Fundamentals
Ideal if you're a: Fund manager, macro analyst
How markets move, what drives sentiment, how to filter signal from noise. Taught from the desk of someone reading this every day.
Stock Market Basics
Ideal if you're a: Equity analyst, research head
P/E ratios, earnings reports, reading a balance sheet. The toolkit a retail investor needs — explained by someone using it professionally.
Retirement Planning
Ideal if you're a: CFP, pension specialist, RM
NPS, PPF, EPF, corpus calculations. The conversations most people delay until it's almost too late — you know how to make them urgent and actionable.
Goal-Based Investing
Ideal if you're a: Wealth planner, CFP, advisor
Mapping investments to real goals — home, education, capital. You've built these plans for clients. Now help a room of people build their own.
