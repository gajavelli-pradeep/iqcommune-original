Teaching modules

Six modules. Teach one or several.

Each module maps to a distinct area of active practice. You can apply for one module or several — if your expertise spans a natural pairing, those two can be bundled as a single 6-hour session. We'll discuss and confirm what makes sense.

Foundations of Personal Finance
Budgeting frameworks, net worth, emergency funds, insurance, and tax basics. Teaching people to get their foundation right before they invest anything. The conversation a financial planner or IFA has with every new client — opened up to a room of twenty-five.
Retirement & Goal-Based Financial Planning
Corpus calculation, NPS/EPF/PPF, and mapping investments to real goals — home, education, retirement. The plan a CFP or pension specialist builds for a client nearing a milestone — now for a room of twenty-five.
Equity Investing Simplified
How markets work, reading financials, valuation ratios, and earnings reports — the toolkit a retail investor needs. Taught by an active equity analyst, not a textbook.
Debt & Fixed Income Investing
Bonds, G-Secs, debt mutual funds, yield, duration, and tax treatment. The fixed income universe decoded by an active portfolio manager.
Asset Allocation & Portfolio Construction
How to split a portfolio across asset classes, build for risk profile, and rebalance. Taught by someone actively making these decisions for real clients — a portfolio manager or wealth manager.
Investment Solutions & Portfolio Strategies
Mutual fund categories, SIP/STP/SWP mechanics, PMS and AIF, portfolio review frameworks, and tax-efficient investing. Taught by a practising portfolio manager.
Applying for two related modules? They can be bundled.
Three natural pairings run back-to-back as a single 6-hour session. If your expertise spans a pair, indicate that in your application and we'll discuss it.
